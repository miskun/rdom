# Bundled font

`unscii-16.hex` is **unscii-16** by Viznut, an 8×16 bitmap font (a few
glyphs 16×16), in GNU Unifont's `.hex` format: one glyph per line,
`codepoint:bitmap`, 32 hex digits for an 8×16 glyph and 64 for a 16×16 one.

Source: <http://viznut.fi/unscii/unscii-16.hex>, fetched 2026-11-07,
SHA-256 `2642c8b748fa81f24d76772d70c55faa720d98fadfec8133daf89136c5c8bfb1`.

License: the unscii variants other than `unscii-16-full` are in the
**public domain** (the project page: "'unscii-16-full' falls under GPL
because of how Unifont is licensed; the other variants are in the Public
Domain."). This is `unscii-16`, not `unscii-16-full`.

rdom-capture's rasterizer draws text with it; nothing else of unscii is
bundled.
